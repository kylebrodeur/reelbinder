# The Bounty Hunter presentation project

Download the [ReelBinder presentation project](https://storage.googleapis.com/reelbinder-public-downloads/v07/ReelBinder.reelbinder.zip) or [watch/download the V07 film](https://storage.googleapis.com/reelbinder-public-downloads/v07/the-bounty-hunter-v07.mp4).

The project includes the screenplay, production planning, floor plan and 72.75-second finished film arranged as 19 source-trim excerpts. The soundtrack is baked into the movie. Historical takes and separate audio stems are omitted from this presentation copy.

Open Studio, choose **ReelBinder project menu → Open script or project archive**, and select the downloaded ZIP. Keep the tab open while the media uploads. Then check the project title and play the film in Render. A fresh-session hosted check completed the media upload in about 56 seconds; your upload speed will affect that time.

The accepted film retains some Rusty dialogue-sync and entrance-transition imperfections.

Adapted from *The Bounty Hunter* by Ministry of Cinema. Screenplay by Bradley Weatherholt. Film and production material rights belong to Kyle Brodeur. ReelBinder's software MIT license does not license the included production materials.
