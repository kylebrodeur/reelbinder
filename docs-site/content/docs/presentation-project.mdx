# The Bounty Hunter presentation project

Two public paths are offered. The **planning study** is a source-only archive with screenplay, production planning, and reference material. The **finished presentation Project** is a media-light archive that opens immediately in Studio; its approved film media stays offline until you explicitly choose **Download media and relink** in Render.

Direct links to unreleased final film bytes are not published from this repository. The approved public media objects and their verified hashes are listed only after rights approval and public-gate review.

Adapted from *The Bounty Hunter* by Ministry of Cinema. Screenplay by Bradley Weatherholt. Film and production material rights belong to Kyle Brodeur. ReelBinder's software MIT license does not license the included production materials.

## Planning study

Download the source-only [Bounty Hunter planning study](https://storage.googleapis.com/reelbinder-public-downloads/starter/bounty-hunter-planning-study.reelbinder.zip). It contains the screenplay, planning setups and floor-plan reference, but no generated takes or finished film. Studio keeps an identical checksum-pinned same-origin copy for its built-in demo; this GCP object is the portable direct-download mirror. The study ships as a `.reelbinder.zip` project archive.

## Finished presentation Project

The finished presentation Project opens as a media-light `.reelbinder.zip` archive. Its timeline, screenplay, production planning, and floor plan load immediately, but the approved film media referenced by the archive is not downloaded until you choose **Download media and relink** in Render. Each media object is fetched from a verified public GCP object, checked against its published SHA-256, byte size, and MIME type, and imported into your visitor session before the Project links to it. No provider job is created, no credentials are used, and no automatic retry occurs.
