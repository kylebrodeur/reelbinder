## Interpretation

Use status words narrowly:

- **Built** means the production artifact was produced from the recorded source.
- **Deployed** means the recorded artifact is active on the target infrastructure.
- **Hosted-verified** means an external client passed the listed HTTPS and behavior checks.
- Creative review, rights approval, and submission acceptance are separate decisions and should not be inferred from technical checks.

If an operator cannot supply an exact identity or check, mark it `not-run` or open a blocker. Do not replace missing evidence with an assumption.
